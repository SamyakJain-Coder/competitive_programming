## Observations
- Distance of the point must be R
- X and Y are constrained

## Approach
- Any point at a distance X+R and X-R is at a distance of R from X and Y (Note : Y not changed)
- Important point to consider is the limits of them , so check if X+R is beyond the constraint if yes then X-R

## Complexity
Time : O(1) , if test cases loop considered : O(T)
